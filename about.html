Our story

Built in Kashmir, calibrated for national-level exams

We combine university-grade academic depth with the discipline of a test-prep studio: clear roadmaps, honest diagnostics, and mentors who stay until the concept is owned.

Vision

Whether a student is aiming for KU’s Integrated Data Science & AI degree, GATE, GAT-B, or BALLB, the same muscle is trained: structured thinking under time pressure.

Founder & mentors

Teaching philosophy

GasshCode’s faculty mix academic research, classroom coaching, and industry engineering. We do not sell shortcuts. We sell sequences.

Founding mentor

Computer science and applied mathematics background with a focus on entrance diagnostics. Philosophy: if a student cannot explain a topic in two minutes, it is not revision-ready.

Quantitative faculty

Specialists in probability, discrete math, and GATE engineering mathematics. Every lecture ends with a 12-minute speed set.

Domain mentors

Law aptitude, life-sciences, and programming lab coaches who run small-group clinics rather than one-way lectures.

Methodology

Three steps. No theatre.

1

Core fundamentals

Concept maps, first-principles notes, and low-stakes drills until definitions are automatic.

2

Problem solving

Pattern libraries, mixed-topic sets, and live whiteboard doubts. Accuracy before speed.

3

Mock tests & time management

Full papers, error logs, and rank-style feedback. Students leave knowing which 20 marks they can still recover.
