FAQ

Common questions

How are fees structured?

Program fees are available on enquiry and include classes, mock series, and digital notes. Books and optional crash revisions may be billed separately. Early-bird offers are available on selected batches until batch lock.

What are typical batch timings?

Evening weekday batches (5:00–7:30 PM) for school/college students, plus weekend intensives. Exact calendars are shared at enrolment and on the course details page.

How does online access work?

Live sessions run on a private classroom link. Recordings stay available for 48 hours. Doubt rooms remain open on WhatsApp for enrolled students.

What study materials are included?

Module notes, formula sheets, PYQ compilations, and mock solutions. Printed packs are available at the Srinagar centre.
