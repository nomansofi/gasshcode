KU Integrated Data Science & AI: 2027 Entrance Syllabus

5-Year Integrated Masters Programme (FYIMP in DS & AI), effective from 2027 · Prepared by GasshCode Academy

Exam pattern

60MCQs
1Mark each
15Units
4Marks per unit

Each unit is tested with multiple-choice questions that have one correct or most appropriate answer, set separately for each section so the whole syllabus is represented uniformly.

Syllabus by unit

UnitTopicsMarks
I Logical ReasoningReasoning

Analogies, syllogisms, statement and conclusions, statement and arguments, blood relations, direction sense, seating arrangements (linear and circular), puzzles, Venn diagrams, coding-decoding, arithmetic number series.

 4
II English Language and ComprehensionLanguage

Vocabulary, spot the error, fill in the blanks, synonyms/homonyms, antonyms, spellings, idioms and phrases, one-word substitution, active/passive voice, direct/indirect narration, reading comprehension.

 4
III Sets, Relations and FunctionsMathematics

Definition and types of sets, operations on sets, types of relations and functions, domain, range and codomain, composite and inverse functions.

 4
IV CalculusMathematics

Limits and continuity, differentiation, application of derivatives, integration and differential equations.

 4
V Statistics and ProbabilityMathematics

Conditional probability, random variables and distributions; data representation through graphs and diagrams (bar charts, pie charts, histograms, frequency polygons, ogives); measures of central tendency (mean, median, mode, geometric and harmonic mean); combined and weighted mean.

 4
VI AlgebraMathematics

Quadratic equations and roots, sequences and series, matrices and determinants: properties and applications.

 4
VII Computer NetworksComputer Science

Types of networks (LAN, MAN, WAN, PAN), topologies and network structures, basics of IP addressing, internet and web communication, browsers, email and messaging tools.

 4
VIII Programming ConceptsComputer Science

Basic programming concepts and introduction to algorithms, data types, control structures (if, for, while), functions and recursion.

 4
IX Data Structures and Data HandlingComputer Science

Linear data structures: arrays (1-D and 2-D), matrices, special and sparse matrices; traversal, linear and binary search, bubble sort; stacks (push, pop) and queues (enqueue, dequeue).

 4
X Boolean AlgebraComputer Science

Basics of Boolean algebra, common logic gates (AND, OR, NOT, XOR), simple circuit design and truth tables.

 4
XI Object-Oriented Programming BasicsComputer Science

Procedural programming versus OOP, benefits of OOP, abstraction, encapsulation, inheritance, polymorphism, C versus C++; C++ elements: tokens, identifiers, variables, constants, data types, streams, operators, decision and loop control structures.

 4
XII Operating System BasicsComputer Science

Types of operating systems, functions (process, memory, file and device management), 32-bit and 64-bit systems, firmware, Windows features and versions, Linux, Android, macOS, GUI and kernel.

 4
XIII Python Programming BasicsComputer Science

Overview of Python: features, history and applications in web development, data science and automation; basic syntax, variables and data types, operators.

 4
XIV Computer FundamentalsComputer Science

Number systems (binary, octal, decimal, hexadecimal) and conversions, binary arithmetic and complements, data representation, range, accuracy, underflow and overflow.

 4
XV Introduction to Emerging TechnologiesComputer Science

Big data, artificial intelligence, machine learning, natural language processing, AR/VR, robotics, Internet of Things and cloud computing with its service types.

 4
