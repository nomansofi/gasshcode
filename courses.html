5-Year Integrated Data Science & AI Entrance (KU)

Preparation for the University of Kashmir 5-year Integrated Masters in Data Science & AI, built on the revised syllabus effective from 2027. Every one of the 15 official units is taught, drilled and tested.

Objectives: secure full command of all 15 units, build accuracy and speed on a 60-question paper, and strengthen computing literacy for the five-year pathway.

₹***** · 12 weeks · Offline / Online

Early bird access: ₹1,499

Module-by-module curriculum (15 units)

Reasoning & language: I Logical Reasoning · II English Language and Comprehension.
Mathematics: III Sets, Relations and Functions · IV Calculus · V Statistics and Probability · VI Algebra.
Computer science & technology: VII Computer Networks · VIII Programming Concepts · IX Data Structures and Data Handling · X Boolean Algebra · XI Object-Oriented Programming Basics · XII Operating System Basics · XIII Python Programming Basics · XIV Computer Fundamentals · XV Emerging Technologies.

Exam pattern & weightage

60 multiple-choice questions of one mark each, with one correct or most appropriate answer. All 15 units carry equal weightage of 4 marks, so no unit can be skipped. Pattern per the University of Kashmir syllabus effective from 2027.

Class schedules & batch timings

Weekday evening: Mon–Fri 5:00–7:00 PM (Hazratbal). Weekend online: Sat–Sun 10:00 AM–1:00 PM. Extra mock Sundays twice a month.

GATE 2027

GATE Computer Science & Data Science

A nine-month subject cycle covering GATE CS and overlapping GATE DA mathematics, with weekly tests that mimic IIT scoring pressure.

Objectives: complete core CS syllabus, raise accuracy under negative marking, and produce a revision notebook before December.

₹***** · 9 months · Hybrid

Module-by-module curriculum

Engineering maths → DSA & algorithms → Digital logic & COA → OS → DBMS → Networks → TOC/compilers → Discrete math → ML/stats for DA → test series.

Exam pattern & weightage

GA 15 · Engineering maths ~13 · Core CS remaining 72. Mix of MCQ, MSQ, and NAT. We train all three formats every week.

Class schedules & batch timings

Tue/Thu/Sat 6:00–8:30 PM live. Recorded backups for 48 hours. Monthly 3-hour CBT mock at the centre or remotely.
